## English

- Plan completion pauses recording and offers session details or manual continuation in the same record. Decision time is excluded. Pending choices survive restart; failed saves can be retried.
- The connection/devices page offers fold/unfold only for equipment declaring support, with confirmation, feedback and timeout refresh. Movement, unknown state and pending feedback block repeated commands.
- Fixes AT-R subtype 1 revolution totals and 1–24 writes, declared zero resistance and calculation inputs, fractional increments, per-field freshness, partial updates, counter rollover and startup ordering.
- The completion checkmark is centered to the right of the heading/subtitle block. Layouts support narrow screens, large fonts, landscape, tablets and dark mode.

See [validation and limits](../V034_VALIDATION.md), [device support](../COMPATIBILITY.md#english) and [workout notes](../TRAINING.md#english). Database version 9 preserves existing records; session CSV format is now 7. Export a full backup before upgrading if desired; new exports require a compatible importer. Regular and Debug variants retain separate installations and data.
