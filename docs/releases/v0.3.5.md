# v0.3.5

[下载普通版 / Download regular APK](https://github.com/q1ngyang/open-mobifitness/releases/download/v0.3.5/OpenMOBI-0.3.5.apk) · [English](#english)

- 修复断开后的器材或心率带被附近设备列表隐藏的问题。重新连接优先重新发现设备，再建立新连接；扫描、连接、服务发现和初始化均有明确的结束条件，允许一次有界恢复，取消后不再继续。
- 重连使用目标地址过滤，避免无过滤扫描在熄屏时被系统暂停。器材和心率带共用扫描频率限制；迟到的扫描结果、连接回调和排队读写不会干扰新连接。
- 后台连接保护覆盖连接准备、仅查看数据、训练、暂停及心率带单独连接。连接全部断开后释放唤醒保护；仍有连接时不会因结束记录而提前停止服务。服务启动失败或异常退出时关闭蓝牙连接，保留并暂停未结束的记录。
- V1、V2、FTMS、Huantong 与心率服务均检查实际运动／心率通知是否订阅成功，电量、档位、控制反馈或器材状态通知不能代替运动数据订阅。失败会释放连接并提示重连；已正确订阅但暂时静止的器材继续等待数据。
- 诊断增加连接阶段、扫描统计、应用前后台、前台服务、唤醒及系统电源状态。GATT 操作异常会结束连接并清理等待状态。

本次修复应用于共用连接层，覆盖椭圆机、自行车、划船机、跑步机、跳绳、哑铃及心率带；不会在重连时自动恢复训练或重放运动／展开收起指令。没有新增型号的硬件支持承诺。

沿用 v0.3.4 的数据库版本 9、训练 CSV 格式 7 和普通版／Debug 版包名、签名，可覆盖升级。独立蓝牙测试包与普通版使用不同包名，数据仍独立。

## English

- Disconnected equipment and heart-rate straps remain visible in nearby results. Reconnection rediscovers the target before creating a fresh connection, with bounded setup stages, one recovery attempt and cancellation.
- Target-address filters keep reconnect scans eligible while the screen is off. Equipment and heart-rate clients share a scan budget. Late callbacks and queued operations cannot migrate to a replacement connection.
- Background protection covers setup, idle live readings, workouts, pauses and heart-only connections. Wake protection ends when both links close. Ending a record cannot stop the service while a link remains active. Service startup failure or destruction closes unprotected links and pauses an unfinished record.
- V1, V2, FTMS, Huantong and heart-rate initialization require successful measurement notification subscriptions. Battery, resistance, control or status notifications cannot conceal measurement subscription failure. Quiet, correctly subscribed equipment continues waiting for data without forced reconnection.
- Diagnostics include setup stages, scan statistics, app visibility, foreground-service lifecycle, wake ownership and power state. GATT operation exceptions close the connection and clear pending state.

Shared fixes cover ellipticals, bikes, rowers, treadmills, jump ropes, dumbbells and heart-rate straps. Reconnection does not automatically resume workouts or replay motion/folding commands. This release does not claim new model-specific hardware certification.

Database version 9, session CSV format 7, regular/Debug package IDs and signing identities remain compatible with v0.3.4. The separate Bluetooth test app retains its own package and data.
